### Added
- First version: Markdown tables in Live Preview and Reading view become Notion-style tables. Click a cell to edit, Enter / Tab to move, add rows and columns, row and column handles plus a right-click menu, drag to resize columns, seven cell backgrounds, three styles and two row spacings; on mobile, tap to edit and long-press for the menu. Select a range to color, align or clear it at once; copy and paste with Excel, Feishu and the web; merge cells; drag row and column handles to reorder; sticky headers on long tables and scroll hints on wide ones. Files stay plain Markdown tables, with widths, colors and merges in one comment below the table.
